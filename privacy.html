NotebookApp Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Google account and Google Drive

NotebookApp allows users to connect their own Google account for synchronization and backup.

NotebookApp requests access only to its private application data area in the user's Google Drive using the Google Drive app data scope.

NotebookApp does not request access to the user's general Google Drive files, photos, documents, or other personal Drive content.

Data stored

Notebook content and synchronization metadata may be stored locally on the user's device and in NotebookApp's private app data folder in the user's own Google Drive account.

Data sharing

NotebookApp does not sell user data and does not share notebook content with advertisers.

Data deletion

Users can delete notebooks from within the application. Synced deletion state may also be stored in the user's Google Drive app data in order to synchronize deletions between the user's devices.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support email shown on the Google authorization screen.
